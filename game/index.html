📜 How to Play

Objective

First player to reach 150 points in their safe Score Tally wins!

138-Card Custom Deck Breakdown

  • 2 through 5: 12 copies each (48 cards) • Face value (2–5 pts)
  • 6 through 9: 10 copies each (40 cards) • Face value (6–9 pts)
  • 10, J, Q, K: 8 copies each (32 cards) • 10 pts each
  • Aces (A): 12 copies (12 cards) • 15 pts each!
  • Jokers (JKR): 6 copies • 0 pts (Wild for Runs)

On Your Turn

  • Draw cards one by one to build your hand.
  • Bust Rule: Drawing a duplicate rank or 2 Jokers ends your turn instantly and forfeits all drawn cards.

Pending Piles & Stealing

  • When you bank your turn, your cards move to your Pending Pile.
  • Pending cards convert to your safe Score Tally at the start of your next turn.
  • If an opponent draws a card matching a rank in your Pending Pile, they steal those cards into their pile upon banking!

Straights / Runs

  • A sequence of 5+ consecutive ranks forms a Run.
  • When banking a Run, you choose:
    1. Score Immediately: Double run points straight to safe Tally.
    2. Move to Pending: Move cards to Pending (unscored) to help steal or defend.

Gems & Safeguards

  • Busting on 3 or fewer cards grants a Pity Gem.
  • If you have 2+ Gems when drawing a bust card, spend 2 Gems to cancel and discard the drawn card!
  • Accumulating 3 Gems automatically converts into +50 Bonus Points.

House Rules

The goal

Be the first to the target score (150 by default). Your score is safe. Your pending pile is not: it becomes score at the start of your next turn, and until then other players can steal from it.

The deck (138 cards)

  • 2 through 5: twelve of each, worth face value
  • 6 through 9: ten of each, worth face value
  • 10, J, Q, K: eight of each, worth 10
  • Aces: twelve, worth 15
  • Jokers: six, worth 0, wild in runs

Your turn

  • Any cards in your pending pile are banked into your score first.
  • Then draw one card at a time. Hit for another, or bank to stop.
  • Draw a rank you already hold and you bust: the whole hand is lost and your turn ends.
  • Two jokers in one hand is a double joker bust: the hand is lost and your score resets to 0.
  • When you bank, the cards in your hand go to your pending pile (jokers are discarded).

Stealing

If your hand holds a rank that also sits in an opponent's pending pile, banking steals those cards into your own pending pile. The bank button shows what you would take, and the gold cells under the table mark ranks that would steal.

Gems

  • Bust on three cards or fewer and you receive a pity gem.
  • Hold 2 gems and you may escape a bust: spend both, discard the bust card, and keep drawing.
  • Hold 3 gems and they convert at once into a 50-point bonus.

Runs

Five or more cards in sequence make a run; jokers fill gaps. A run may be high (10-J-Q-K-A) or low (A-2-3-4-5) but never wraps. When you bank with a run you choose: score the run immediately at double value, or hold the whole hand as pending unscored.

The table strip

The row of ranks beneath the table shows red for ranks you hold (a bust if drawn) and gold for ranks that would steal. In blind play the small number is how many of that rank are face up in hands and pending piles. With card counting on, it is how many remain in the deck.

Turn order

The first game follows seat order, or a shuffled order if that option is on. In every rematch the winner of the previous game deals first and the order stays the same.

Ending the game

  • Final round (default): when a player reaches the target, every other player takes one last turn. All pending piles are then banked and the highest score wins.
  • Instant win: the first player to reach the target wins on the spot.

Optional rules

  • Last call (final round only, on by default): once the final round begins, escaping a bust costs 1 gem instead of 2.
  • Shuffled seating: randomize the order for the first game.
  • Bust odds: show the chance that the next card busts you.
  • Card counting: show how many of each rank remain in the deck, and open the deck and discard inspectors.

Keyboard

H to hit, S to bank, Enter to continue after a bust, Y or N to answer the gem prompt, L for the log, Esc to open or close the menu.

Escape for two gems?

Spend 2 gems to discard the bust card and keep drawing.

Score it now at double value, or hold the whole hand as pending.

📖 Official Game Rules (Click to View)
  • Objective: Reach 150 Safe Tally points first to win.
  • Duplicate Card Bust: Drawing a duplicate rank busts your turn.
  • Double Joker Bust: Drawing 2 Jokers in 1 turn triggers a Double Bust! Your entire Safe Tally wipes to 0 (cannot be saved with Gems).
  • Gem System (Max 2): You can hold up to 2 Gems (`2/3`).
  • 3rd Gem Conversion: Earning your 3rd Gem resets Gems to 0 and adds +50 Bonus Points directly to your Tally! (Can trigger an instant win!).
  • Gem Saves: Spend 2 Gems to save a normal bust.
  • Double Bust Pity Gem: A Double Bust automatically awards 1 Gem regardless of cards drawn.

📜 Rules of Play

Objective

First player to reach 150 points in safe Score Tally wins!

138-Card Deck Breakdown

  • 2 through 5: 12 copies each (48 cards) • 2–5 pts
  • 6 through 9: 10 copies each (40 cards) • 6–9 pts
  • 10, J, Q, K: 8 copies each (32 cards) • 10 pts each
  • Aces (A): 12 copies (12 cards) • 15 pts each
  • Jokers (JKR): 6 copies • Wilds for Runs (0 pts)

On Your Turn

  • Draw cards sequentially. Drawing a matching rank causes a standard Bust.
  • Double Bust (2 Jokers): Instantly resets safe Score Tally to 0! Jokers can never be banked or stolen.

Pending Piles & Stealing

  • Banked non-Joker cards go to your Pending Pile, converting to safe Tally at the start of your next turn.
  • Drawing cards matching an opponent's Pending cards lets you steal them when you bank!

Gems & Runs

  • Busting early (3 or fewer cards) gives 1 Pity Gem. Spend 2 Gems to cancel a drawn bust card!
  • 5+ card runs can be doubled directly to safe score Tally or sent unscored to Pending.
